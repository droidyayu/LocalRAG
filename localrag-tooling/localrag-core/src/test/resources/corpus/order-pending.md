## Common reasons
An order stays pending when the market has not reached your limit price, when the instrument is
in a circuit or auction period, or when the exchange session has not opened yet.

## What to check
Open the order and compare its limit price with the last traded price. If they are far apart the
order will wait until the market moves.

## Cancelling a pending order
You can cancel any pending order before it fills. Cancellation is immediate and no charge applies.
