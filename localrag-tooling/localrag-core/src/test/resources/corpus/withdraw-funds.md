## Placing a withdrawal
Withdrawals go to a verified bank account in your own name. Request one from the funds screen.

## How long it takes
Most withdrawals settle within two working days. Requests made outside banking hours start the
next working day.

## Why a withdrawal may be held
A withdrawal is held while KYC is incomplete, or while the amount is still tied up as margin on
open positions.
