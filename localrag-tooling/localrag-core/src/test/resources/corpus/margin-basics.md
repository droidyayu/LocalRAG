## What margin is
Margin is the collateral held against an open leveraged position. It is not a fee and it is
released when the position closes.

## Margin level
Margin level is your equity divided by the margin in use, shown as a percentage. A higher number
means more headroom.

## Margin calls
If margin level falls too far the position may be closed automatically to protect the account.
