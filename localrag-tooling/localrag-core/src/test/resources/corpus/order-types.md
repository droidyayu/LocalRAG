## Market orders
A market order fills at the best price available right now. Use it when getting filled matters
more than the exact price.

## Limit orders
A limit order fills only at your price or better. It may not fill at all if the market never
reaches your price.

## Stop loss orders
A stop loss order becomes active once the market trades through your stop price, and is used to
cap a loss on an open position.
