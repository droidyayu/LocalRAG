## Choosing a risk level
Each portfolio is labelled low, moderate or high risk, describing how much its value is expected
to move rather than how likely it is to lose money.

## Adding and withdrawing
You can add at any time. Withdrawals settle after the portfolio rebalances, which can take a few
working days.
