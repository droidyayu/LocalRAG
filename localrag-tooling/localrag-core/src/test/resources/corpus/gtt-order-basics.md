## What it does
A GTT order stays pending until your trigger price is reached. Nothing is sent to the exchange
until then, so the order does not consume margin while it waits.

## How to place one
1. Open the instrument page and choose GTT.
2. Set the trigger price and the limit price.
3. Review the order and confirm.

## How long it lasts
A GTT order remains active for one year from the day you place it. After that it expires and you
have to place it again.
