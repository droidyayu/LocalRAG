## Brokerage
Brokerage is charged per executed order. Cancelled and rejected orders are never charged.

## Exchange and regulatory charges
Exchange turnover charges and regulatory levies are collected on your behalf and passed through
without a markup.

## Where to see what you paid
Every contract note itemises brokerage and each pass through charge separately.
