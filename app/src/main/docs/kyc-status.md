## Where to check
Your KYC status is shown at the top of the profile screen. Verified, In review and Action needed
are the three states you will see.

## In review
A submitted document is usually reviewed within two working days. You cannot trade until the
review completes.

## Action needed
This means a document was unclear or expired. Open the KYC screen to see which one and upload a
replacement.
