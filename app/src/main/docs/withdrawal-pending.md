## Processing window
A withdrawal requested after the daily cut off is processed on the next working day.

## Available balance versus equity
Only the available balance can be withdrawn. Funds held as margin against open positions are not
available until those positions close.

## Bank side delays
Once sent, arrival depends on the receiving bank. Ask them for the reference on the payout advice.
