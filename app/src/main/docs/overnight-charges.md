## When it applies
Financing applies to leveraged positions held past the daily rollover time. Positions closed the
same day are not financed.

## How it is calculated
Financing is applied to the full notional value of the position, not to the margin you posted.

## Where it appears
Financing shows as a separate line on the account statement each day it applies.
