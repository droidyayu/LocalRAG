## Insufficient funds
The most common rejection is not enough available balance to cover the order value plus charges.

## Instrument restrictions
Some instruments are blocked for intraday trading or restricted by the exchange on a given day.

## Price band breaches
An order priced outside the daily price band is rejected by the exchange, not by us.
